# Primitive soup

**Symptom.** The model reads as boxes, balls and cones pushed together instead of one form.

**Cause.** Building organic shapes (faces, bodies) from primitives, even with blending.

**Fix.** Organic shapes from Hunyuan (a clean concept image), then cleaned in the `blender` server. In code: one metaball or voxel-remeshed solid per moving part, not separate primitives.
