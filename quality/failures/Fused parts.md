# Fused parts

**Symptom.** Parts that should move separately come out as one piece (nine tails in one sheet).

**Cause.** Generators fuse anything that touches in the input image.

**Fix.** Generate moving parts separately, or from an image where they're apart; split and rig them in Blender.
