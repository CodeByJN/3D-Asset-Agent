# Awkward joints

**Symptom.** Rigid pieces meet with a visible seam or gap, especially when they bend.

**Cause.** Rigid parts pivoting about joints without overlap; no skinning.

**Fix.** Overlap round volumes at every joint, or rig with bones and skin weights (one smooth mesh).
