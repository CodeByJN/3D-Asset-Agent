# Missing markings

**Symptom.** The signature graphics aren't there or are too thin to read: eye bands, seals, stripes.

**Cause.** Markings as thin geometry, or forgotten in an alternate form.

**Fix.** Paint them on the surface (kit `paint`/`decal`) bold enough to read at game distance; check every form.
