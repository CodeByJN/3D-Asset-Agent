# Clipping

**Symptom.** Parts pass through each other (ears through ears, inner black through the outer ear).

**Cause.** Thin parts placed by eye without checking every view; offsets smaller than the folds.

**Fix.** Check front, side and 3/4. Build layered parts flat or with a real gap; inspect after posing too.
