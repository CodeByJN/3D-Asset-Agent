# Flat sheets from painted refs

**Symptom.** Hunyuan returns flat broken slabs instead of a solid model.

**Cause.** Dark, busy painted images or close crops; the subject isn't separable from the background.

**Fix.** Give Hunyuan a clean subject on a plain background, ideally a 3/4 view of the whole thing (a model sheet).
