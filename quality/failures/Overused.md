# Overused

**Symptom.** Players have seen this exact asset in many games; it makes a game look like a free-model build.

**Cause.** Taking the most popular free model instead of making one.

**Fix.** Make our own take: keep what makes the classic work (silhouette, palette) and change shape, proportions
and detail so it's ours. Colour and size variants keep a set from looking copy-pasted.
