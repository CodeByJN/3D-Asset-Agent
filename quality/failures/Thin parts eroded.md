# Thin parts eroded

**Symptom.** Ears, fins and rims shrink to slivers after cleanup.

**Cause.** Heavy smoothing or coarse voxel remesh eats thin plates.

**Fix.** Give thin parts real thickness; smooth them lightly before joining.
