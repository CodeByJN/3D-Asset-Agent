# Face likeness

**Symptom.** The face doesn't look like the character; everything else can be right.

**Cause.** Guessing the face from paintings; building faces and hair from primitives.

**Fix.** Real assets for Roblox characters (`character`); for creatures, a model sheet and a staged review of the head.
