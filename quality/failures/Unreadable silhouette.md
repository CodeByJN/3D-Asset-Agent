# Unreadable silhouette

**Symptom.** You can't tell what the object is: it's lost in glow, effects or a dark, low-contrast palette.

**Cause.** Effects covering the model; one dark tone everywhere.

**Fix.** The model must read as a flat black silhouette first; effects and glow go around it, not over it. Keep value contrast between parts.
