# VFX overpower the mesh

**Symptom.** Glow, auras and particles drown the model; a good mesh grades 2-4 points lower than it would bare.

**Cause.** Effects sized and coloured to impress on their own, not to frame the object.

**Fix.** VFX accent the mesh: keep its silhouette and colours readable, effects smaller and thinner than the object,
brightest at edges and tips. Grade the bare mesh first, then check the effects add to it.
