# Spike clutter

**Symptom.** Fur, tufts or spikes everywhere; the silhouette gets noisy and cheap.

**Cause.** Adding a cone for every tuft.

**Fix.** A few large, swept tufts where the reference has them (cheeks, chest, tail tips). Smooth everywhere else.
