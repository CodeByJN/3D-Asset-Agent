# Rating scale

Stars are 1-10 (half stars allowed). Rate what you'd ship, not effort. Verdicts: **good** 8+, **mixed** 5.5-7.5,
**bad** 5 or less.

| Stars | Means | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | Better than you'd expect from a paid asset; nothing to change. | `rei_treasure`, `rei_explosion_a` (VFX); Gothic Dragon Statue (store): clean, fits Roblox |
| 9 | Ship it; small nitpicks only. | [[master_sword v5]]: sword, lighting and VFX great; stones could be better. Fantasy Monster Pack, Dragon Arch Portal (store): great, a little out of place in Roblox |
| 8 | Good. One clear improvement, but you'd use it. | Dragon Heart Spawner (store): a bit too much detail |
| 7 | Reads correctly and is clean, but plain or missing the detail that makes it special. | Sol's RNG auras plateaued here ([[VFX]]); Demon dragon monster (store): not bad, nothing crazy |
| 6 | Right idea, visible problems a player would notice. | [[kurama v8]] (6.5): animations good, face reads as shapes thrown together |
| 5 | The concept is there; several things are wrong. | [[kurama v4]]: too spiky, awkward joints, ears clipping |
| 3-4 | Recognisable but wrong in the ways that matter (face, proportions, silhouette). | [[gon_kid v2]], [[gon_adult v1]] |
| 1-2 | Broken or not what was asked for. | Stud-style dragon pets (store): see [[Taste]] |

When you rate, say what works and what's wrong (the review window has fields for both). The words matter more
than the number: they become the `What works` / `What's wrong` sections every later version is checked against.

Sub-scores, style score, genres and the quick chat format: [[Grading guide]].
