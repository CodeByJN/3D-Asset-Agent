# Buildings

**Reference (10/10): Fantasy Medieval House Pack - Village Buildings** (Creator Store 139701335911079). Build
houses from it and take inspiration from it:
- Stylised, chunky proportions: thick walls, oversized roofs, slight exaggeration, not realistic.
- Each house has its own strong, saturated roof colour (red, orange, purple, blue) over warm stone and timber.
- Every surface textured: shingles, stone bases, timber framing, windows and doors with depth.
- A set of variants that belong together.

Also good: Medieval House Knight (8, "pushing a 9"): detailed half-timbered house with stone base and towers;
Stylized Fantasy House (8): well modelled, but fits few games (a background house).

Avoid:
- Flat colours and boxes: plain walls, a single-colour roof, no texture ([[Flat untextured]]); "My first fantasy
  house" (0) and the bright blue-roof box (1, "don't create anything that looks like that").
- Tilted or crooked "whimsical" houses: only for horror ([[Taste]]).
- The classic Roblox house: iconic but the most overused there is (3) ([[Overused]]).

Technical: [[Roblox technical]]. Packs are graded as a set.
