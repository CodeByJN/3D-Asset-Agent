# Characters (R15)

Player-sized characters and NPCs. What reviewers judge first is the **face and hair**: get those wrong and nothing
else saves it ([[gon_kid v2]], 4: "clothes and animation good; face and hair ugly, nothing like Gon").

1. **Likeness**: face, hair and colours read as the character at a glance, next to the reference.
2. **A normal Roblox body**: R15 proportions, so every R15 animation and accessory fits ([[gon_adult v1]]: "keep a
   normal Roblox body shape").
3. **Hair flows and leaves the face visible** ([[gon_adult v1]]: "hair too stiff and covers the face").
4. **Clothes** match the outfit and move with the body.

## Method
- Known characters: compose real Creator Store assets (`character`): clothing, hair and faces on R15. Don't
  kit-build faces or hair from primitives (see [[Primitive soup]]).
- Show the face before anything else and get a yes. See [[Staged review]].
