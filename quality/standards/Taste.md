# Taste

What the reviewer's grades say beyond the rubrics, drawn from Creator Store grading (tags in brackets).

- **Fits Roblox.** The top grade goes to models that look clean and belong in a Roblox game. Excellent models
  that feel out of place (realistic monsters, ornate portals) top out at 9 [`roblox-fit-low`, `roblox-fit-high`].
- **No stud or blocky style.** Stud and voxel models grade 1-3 however well made; one would be a 7 among stud
  assets [`style-stud`]. Don't build in that style unless a project asks for it.
- **Grade for the genre.** An asset is judged for the games it fits; the note says which [`genre-*` tags:
  traditional, hangout, roleplay, realistic, stud]. Realistic models aren't the default for Roblox but can reach
  9 in realistic games (war sims); they're wrong for brainrot games.
- **Original over overused.** Assets players have seen in many games grade 1 ([[Overused]]); "lacks originality"
  costs a point even on a good one.
- **Too simple is worse than classic.** A bare low-detail version of a classic shape is a 0-1; use the classic
  instead. The classic Roblox tree (7) is the versatile baseline, best with colour and size variants.
- **Clean over busy.** Detail helps until it stops reading; "a bit too much detail" costs a point.
- **Scripts add value.** A model that comes working (AI, attacks) grades above its looks [`scripted`].
- **Weapons should be usable.** A sword that comes with its animations and system is a 10; the bare classic
  Roblox sword (and copies of it) is a 3: classic but not special [`usable`, `classic-basic`].
- **VFX make or break it.** Effects that fit the mesh lift it to 9-10; effects that drown it cost 2-4 points
  however good the mesh is ([[VFX overpower the mesh]]). Without VFX, a great mesh still gets an 8.
- **"Not bad but nothing crazy" is a 7.** Competent and clean isn't enough for 8+; it needs something that stands out.
