# Taste

What the reviewer's grades say beyond the rubrics, drawn from Creator Store grading (tags in brackets).

- **Fits Roblox.** The top grade goes to models that look clean and belong in a Roblox game. Excellent models
  that feel out of place (realistic monsters, ornate portals) top out at 9 [`roblox-fit-low`, `roblox-fit-high`].
- **No stud or blocky style.** Stud and voxel models grade 1-3 however well made; one would be a 7 among stud
  assets [`style-stud`]. Don't build in that style unless a project asks for it.
- **Clean over busy.** Detail helps until it stops reading; "a bit too much detail" costs a point.
- **Scripts add value.** A model that comes working (AI, attacks) grades above its looks [`scripted`].
- **"Not bad but nothing crazy" is a 7.** Competent and clean isn't enough for 8+; it needs something that stands out.
