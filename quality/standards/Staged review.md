# Staged review

For a known character or creature, build and show one stage at a time, side by side with the reference at the same
angle, and get a yes before the next:

1. Head shape (grey, silhouette over the reference)
2. Eyes (brow, sockets, eye shape)
3. Mouth and teeth, nose
4. Markings and colour
5. Body, then tails or other parts
6. Motion

Why: on Kurama, five full passes (5-10 minutes each) drifted from the references before anyone could catch it; a
stage takes a minute and shows mistakes while they're cheap.
