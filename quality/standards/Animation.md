# Animation

R15 presets (`examples/animation/`) and creature moves. Lessons that moved ratings up (also in
`library/anim/presets.json` `_lessons`, which `animate('help')` shows):

1. **Exaggerate**, about 1.5x past what looks correct, and move the whole body, not one limb.
2. **Lead with the hips**; chest, arms and head follow with overlap.
3. **Plant feet** with IK whenever they shouldn't slide.
4. **Splines through poses**; ease in and out only into impacts.
5. **Creatures**: everything idles (tails sway and ripple out to the tip), attacks have anticipation and recoil,
   impacts shake the camera.
